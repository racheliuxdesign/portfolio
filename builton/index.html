Permit to Work

An interactive prototype of the PTW Create → Approve loop, built for the two people at the heart of site safety.

👷 Foreman — create & submit In the field, on a phone. Picks a permit type, fills the form, sets time & place, submits for approval.
🦺 Site Official — review & decide Approves, requests changes, or rejects — with a comment and signature. Legally accountable.

The two roles share one live dataset. Submit as the Foreman, then switch role to review the same permit as the Official. Use the ⇄ button in the top bar to switch roles anytime.
