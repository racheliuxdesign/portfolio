Cloud Resource Scanner

One of the most basic challenges security teams face is simply knowing what cloud resources are deployed across their environments.

Most organizations are connected to multiple Cloud Service Providers, with resources spread across accounts, regions, and services — each with its own conventions and blind spots.

For SOC engineers, the ability to quickly filter and search through this sprawl is the single most important capability — it’s how they triage incidents and find the exact resource that matters in the moment.

This project gives SOC teams a clear, unified view of their cloud resources, with filtering and search at the core of the experience.

The main inventory view: a unified list of cloud resources discovered across all connected providers, giving SOC engineers a single starting point for exploration.

Filtering in action — narrowing down the inventory by the attributes that matter most during triage, such as resource type, provider, account, or region.

Search results surfaced quickly, so SOC engineers can locate the exact resource they need without scanning long, noisy lists.

A focused resource view, consolidating the key metadata an engineer needs to validate exposure and decide on next steps.

Clicking on a tab triggers an auto-scroll to the relevant section, so engineers can quickly jump between areas of the page without losing their place or hunting through a long view.

Cross-cloud visibility brought together in one place, helping teams reason about their environment as a whole rather than provider by provider. [Click image to enlarge]
