Wireframe to final UI

The side-panel is organized as an incident command surface.

Structure

Context never scrolls away before the analyst has enough signal. Evidence pivots are below the AI briefing, so Daniel can investigate without re-opening the alert.

Why dark cyber UI?

Dark surfaces reduce glare in SOC environments and allow severity, confidence, and containment states to stand out with high-contrast color.

Why not auto-remediate?

High-impact identity and device controls require Daniel's review. The system recommends, explains, confirms, and audits rather than silently acting.

Why pivots instead of deep links?

Pivots preserve the primary story: who acted, what changed, why it matters, and what should happen next.
